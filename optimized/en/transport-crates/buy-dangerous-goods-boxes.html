FAQ about buying battery storage and transport boxes

Which dangerous-goods box should I buy for critically defective EV lithium-ion batteries?

For critically defective high-voltage batteries, the SafetyBATTbox XL-2.2+ or XL-lite is recommended. Both models are approved to ADR 2025 and for the packing instructions P911 and LP906 and are designed for large-format traction batteries from electric vehicles. Thanks to the integrated smoke-gas routing and thermal insulation, they offer maximum transport safety even at acute risk of thermal runaway.

What is the difference between the ADR packing instructions P908 and P911?

P908 applies to defective lithium-ion batteries where a thermal runaway is not immediately imminent. P911, by contrast, covers the packing instructions for critically defective batteries where a thermal runaway cannot be ruled out. The requirements for packaging, labelling and documentation are correspondingly stricter under P911 and require specially approved dangerous-goods boxes, which businesses can buy from LogBATT.

Are the dangerous-goods transport boxes approved to ADR 2025 for industrial batteries?

Yes, all SafetyBATTbox models are tested and approved in accordance with the current ADR 2025 regulations. Depending on the model, the approvals cover the packing instructions P908, P911 and LP906. The BAM design-type approval is recognised in Great Britain through the mutual recognition between ADR contracting parties; the GB competent authority is the DfT, with the VCA Dangerous Goods Office responsible for packaging certification. Where required, we provide the corresponding approval documents and test evidence – for a legally secure documentation of dangerous-goods transports.
