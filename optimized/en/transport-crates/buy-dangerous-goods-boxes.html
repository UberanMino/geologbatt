FAQ on buying battery storage and transport containers

Which dangerous goods box should I buy for critically defective lithium-ion batteries from electric vehicles?

For critically defective high-voltage batteries, the SafetyBATTbox XL or XL-Lite is recommended. Both models are approved to ADR 2025 as well as for the packing instructions P911 and LP906 and are designed for large-format traction batteries from electric vehicles. Thanks to integrated flue-gas routing and thermal insulation, they offer maximum transport safety even where there is an acute risk of thermal runaway.

What is the difference between the ADR packing instructions P908 and P911?

P908 applies to defective lithium-ion batteries where thermal runaway is not imminent. P911, on the other hand, comprises packing instructions for critically defective batteries where thermal runaway cannot be ruled out. The requirements for packaging, labelling and documentation are correspondingly stricter under P911 and require specially approved dangerous goods boxes that companies can buy at LogBATT.

Are the dangerous goods transport boxes approved to ADR 2025 for industrial batteries?

Yes, all SafetyBATTbox models are tested and approved to the current ADR 2025 regulations. Depending on the model, the approvals cover the packing instructions P908, P911 and LP906. On request, we are happy to provide the relevant approval documents and test certificates – for legally compliant documentation of dangerous goods transports.
