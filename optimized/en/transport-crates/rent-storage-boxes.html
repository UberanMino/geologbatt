Which standards and regulations must be met when transporting lithium batteries?

For hire containers in which lithium batteries are transported, the provisions of ADR (Class 9 dangerous goods, UN 3480/3481) and, where applicable, the IATA and IMDG regulations for air and sea transport also apply. In Great Britain, ADR is applied to domestic carriage by the Carriage of Dangerous Goods and Use of Transportable Pressure Equipment Regulations 2009 (CDG 2009). The commercial consignor is responsible for the correct classification and labelling of the consignment and must appoint a Dangerous Goods Safety Adviser (DGSA, ADR 1.8.3). Anyone who hires a transport crate for their lithium battery from LogBATT can nevertheless be sure that the container meets the basic requirements and holds a BAM design-type approval recognised in Great Britain.
