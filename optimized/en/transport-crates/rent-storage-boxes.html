Which standards and regulations must be observed when transporting lithium batteries?

ADR regulations (dangerous goods class 9, UN 3480/3481) – in the UK implemented via the Carriage of Dangerous Goods and Use of Transportable Pressure Equipment Regulations 2009 – as well as, where applicable, IATA and IMDG rules for air and sea transport also apply to rented dangerous goods boxes in which lithium batteries are transported. Commercial consignors bear responsibility for the correct classification and labelling of the shipment. However, anyone who rents a transport box for their lithium battery at LogBATT can be sure that the container meets the fundamental requirements.
