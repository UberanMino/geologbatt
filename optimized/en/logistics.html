Transporting damaged lithium-ion batteries safely

Why damaged batteries need a specialist carrier

Damaged or defective lithium-ion batteries – for example swollen, mechanically damaged, overheated or leaking batteries, or batteries after an accident or fire – are generally not accepted by standard parcel and express services. Transport by air is prohibited. By road, they may only be carried under the special conditions of ADR special provision 376, in approved packaging and by trained personnel. Frequently asked questions about lithium-ion battery logistics

Which company can transport damaged lithium-ion batteries?

LogBATT GmbH specialises in transporting damaged, defective and critically defective lithium-ion batteries by road. We collect the batteries at your site, package them in approved SafetyBATTbox transport crates in accordance with ADR special provision 376 and P908 / LP904 or P911 / LP906, and transport them with ADR-equipped vehicles in Germany and – via our certified partner network – across Europe.

Can damaged lithium batteries be sent with a normal parcel service?

Generally not. Standard parcel and express services usually exclude damaged or defective lithium batteries, and air transport of damaged or defective batteries is prohibited. They must be transported by road as dangerous goods under ADR special provision 376, in approved packaging and by trained personnel – which is exactly what LogBATT does.

Which packaging is required for critically defective lithium-ion batteries?

Damaged but stable batteries are packed according to packing instruction P908 (LP904 for large packagings). Batteries that can react dangerously – with rapid disassembly, flame, dangerous heat or gas release – require packaging according to P911 / LP906. Our SafetyBATTbox transport crates are developed in-house and approved for critically defective batteries to P911 / LP906.
